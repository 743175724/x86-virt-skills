## How it was run

- **Answering was blind.** The answerers were given `blind/<type>.yaml`, which
  strips `must_include`, `must_not_include`, `required_citation`, `notes` and
  **the `chapter` field**. The last matters most: routing is part of what is
  measured, so handing over the destination would have measured only whether a
  chapter contains a fact, not whether a reader can reach it.
- **Answering and scoring were done by different agents**, four answerers and
  two scorers, none of whom wrote the skill.
- **Scorers were told to check the specification, not just the key.** Both did:
  one verified four load-bearing trap values against the local PDFs, the other
  checked every load-bearing value in both its categories, including reading
  Tables B-1 and B-2 with column realignment. That is why "the answer matches
  the key" is not the whole basis of the score.

## The key was wrong four times

This is the same rate the sibling Intel run saw, and the reason scorers are
given explicit authority to dispute. Each was confirmed against the PDF.

| Question | Key said | Specification says |
|---|---|---|
| `diag-avic-incomplete-ipi` | invalid **Logical** APIC ID Table entry causes `#VMEXIT` for directed interrupts | APM2 §15.29.6.1: "address in the **Physical APIC table** … For directed interrupts, if the selected table entry is not valid, cause a #VMEXIT" |
| `vnmi-virtualization` | delivered when "`V_NMI` is set, unmasked, no interrupt shadow" | APM2 §15.21.10 lists three conditions and one of them is **"virtual interrupts are enabled with VGIF"**, which the key dropped |
| `vmrun-consistency-checks` | CR4 bits **20–24** are architecturally defined | Figure 3-7 prints CR4 bits **63:24** as Reserved, MBZ; the highest defined bit is 23 (CET) |
| `diag-sev-es-disabled-on-snp-host` | CVE-2025-48517 fixed at 1.37.23 / 1.37.31 / 1.37.41 | AMD-SB-3023 scopes it to **EPYC 9005 Turin only, SEV FW 1.37.41 only**; the three-level list belongs to CVE-2025-48514 |

Two of the four are the *same process failure* rather than research errors: the
chapter had already been corrected and the key had not. `vmrun-consistency-checks`
and `diag-sev-es-disabled-on-snp-host` both encoded a value that verification
had fixed in the corpus days earlier. **When a fact changes, "where else is this
written" is a question that has to be asked deliberately** — a sweep of every
value corrected during verification turned up one more instance, in `errata.md`
E05, which the mechanism answerer noticed independently.

## What the run exposed in the skill

Every genuine miss was **"the fact is present but was not found"**. There were
no wrong-chapter routes among them and exactly one content gap in 80 questions.

- **One real content gap.** `05-exit-decoding.md` did not say that an
  intercepted external or virtual interrupt is *left pending rather than
  consumed*; the fact existed only in `03-intercepts.md` §3.2, and the question
  routes to `05`. A handler that reads `VMEXIT_INTR` as delivery rather than
  notification will re-exit immediately and livelock, so this one had teeth.
  Added to `05` §5.1 against the `60h`/`61h`/`64h` band.
- **A load-bearing fact filed where its reader will not look.** "VMRUN and
  `#VMEXIT` write CR0, CR3, CR4 and EFER but do not flush the TLB" was in
  `06-nested-paging.md` §6.10, *Shadow paging without nested paging* — the one
  section a nested-paging reader skips. It is a property of the world switch,
  not of shadow paging, and now sits in §6.8 with the rest of TLB control.
- **Routing anchors were chapter entry points, not owning sections.** Ten of 24
  lookup answers had to fall back to `00-quickref.md` §0.3 to find the section
  holding the table. Nothing failed because of it, but the table was
  load-bearing on an undocumented second lookup. The rows now name the owning
  section, and where a chapter has several, each is labelled with what it holds.
- **`00-quickref.md` §0.2 was thin on Windows symptoms.** Six of the twenty
  diagnosis questions had no row at all and were rescued by the main routing
  table; a seventh, "a mitigation disabled SEV-ES", had a row that pointed at
  the wrong owner and was rescued only because `SKILL.md` step 3 makes reading
  `errata.md` mandatory. §0.2 gained a Windows-symptoms block and a
  firmware-disabled-a-feature row.
