## Defects found

### D1 — chapter 16 cannot satisfy its own required citations *(causes the only FAIL)*

Three of the five performance questions demand an Intel anchor that cannot exist:
§16.4 (WSL2 overhead) has no Intel anchor at all, and §16.5 and §16.10.1 are
anchored only to Microsoft `[465]`. A correct, complete, honest answer scores a
citation failure.

This is a conflict between two of the skill's own rules. `SKILL.md`'s output
standard says to reproduce the Intel document and section; its known-limits
section says the Windows chapters cite Microsoft Learn as Tier 2. `lint.mjs`
accepts them because `[465]` resolves. **The eval key is the thing that is
wrong** — you cannot cite Intel for a WSL2 measurement. Fix: state in the output
standard that a platform or measurement claim cites its Tier-2 source with page
or section, and correct those three keys.

### D2 — locatability, not content *(6 of the 16 missed items)*

Every mechanism miss is a fact that exists in the corpus, in a section the owning
section never points at:

| Fact | Lives in | Reader was sent to |
|---|---|---|
| "never advance RIP", bits 3/4/5 triage | `05` §5.3.1, §5.12 | `06` |
| "read IDT-vectoring info on every exit" | `05` §5.12 | `05` §5.6 (owns the field) |
| IR fault reasons 21h / 26h | `11` §11.10 | `11` §11.3, §11.4.1 |
| FB_CLEAR ↔ L1D_FLUSH interaction | `15` §15.4.2 | `15` §15.5 |

Four one-line cross-references recover all six items. This is the failure class
`lint.mjs` structurally cannot catch: every one of these claims is well-formed,
well-anchored and correctly owned. It is the reader's path that is broken.

### D3 — VT-d fault-reason ranges are filed as a "pitfall"

Legacy 1h–Eh, interrupt-remapping 20h–2Bh, RTA 30h–34h, scalable 38h–A5h sit in
`12` §12.9 item 2, roughly 300 lines past the §12.3.1 table. The skill's own stop
rule guarantees a reader never gets there. These ranges are not a pitfall — they
are the first thing a fault decoder needs, because they select the table.

## Measurement caveats

- **The scorer is the agent that answered.** `RUNNER.md` counters this with
  literal scoring and a verbatim quote for every failed item, which narrows the
  bias but does not remove it. Read these numbers as an optimistic bound. An
  unbiased run needs a different model family to score.
- **The lookup agent disclosed partial contamination**: inspecting the YAML with
  `head -40` exposed two questions' expected answers before it answered them.
  Excluding both, that subset is 127/130 · 97.7%. It disclosed this unprompted.
- A reported mojibake in `questions.yaml` was a **false alarm** — `M-BM-'` is the
  correct UTF-8 encoding of `§`, byte-identical to the chapter files.
