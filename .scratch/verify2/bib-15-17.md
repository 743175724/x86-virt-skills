# Proposed bibliography entries for `15-security.md` and `17-windows-platform.md`

Produced by the 2026-09-20 independent verification of chapters 15 and 17 (216 claims).
**`references/bibliography.md` was not edited.** Every entry below is a source that
verification showed is *needed*: either a claim rests on it and no cited entry carries
it, or an existing entry was found not to support the claim attached to it.

IDs come from the free part of the 460–499 block. Taken already: 460–465, 471–472,
474–485. Free and allocated below: **466–470, 473, 486–499** (20 entries, all used).

Until these are merged, every affected claim says in its own text which source actually
carries the fact and carries `C:low`/`C:medium` or `[no-spec]`. **No chapter cites an ID
that does not resolve today.**
