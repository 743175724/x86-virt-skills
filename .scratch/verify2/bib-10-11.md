## Why nothing needed staging

The two chapters are pure specification material: register offsets, bit layouts, descriptor formats,
fault reason codes and enable handshakes. There is no performance number, no Linux `intel-iommu`
behaviour, no vendor blog and no CVE in either file, which is exactly the shape a chapter tracking a
single Intel document should have. The one non-§-anchored fact introduced during this pass (the
Revision History dates for HPT and for the first-stage rename) lives in the front matter of the same
document `[8]` already covers, and is stated in the claim text rather than in the anchor slot.
