## Provenance of the primary sources used (for the audit trail, not for citation)

All three PDFs were downloaded to
`…/scratchpad/sdm/` and text-extracted locally with `pdftotext`, in both `-layout` and `-raw`
reading order, because Appendix A/B/C tables column-desync in layout mode. Order numbers and
revisions were read off each PDF's own cover page rather than assumed.

Vol.4 was used only to cross-check the twenty capability-MSR addresses 480H–493H and
IA32_FEATURE_CONTROL at 3AH against Vol.3D Appendix A; every address agreed, and Vol.4's rows also
supplied the CPUID/BASIC-bit existence gates now recorded in `08-vmx-instructions.md` §8.6.1.

brief that commissioned this pass mis-stated Vol.3D's order number as 326019 (which is Vol.3C). The
URL in the brief resolves correctly, so this is a slip in the brief, not in the bibliography.
