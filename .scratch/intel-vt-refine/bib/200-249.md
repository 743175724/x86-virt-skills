## Notes for the bibliography owner

1. **Entry [21] needs correcting.** The existing entry reads "Order 337679-002, Rev 1.0. 2020-09".
   The PDF served from
   `https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/671403/intel-scalable-io-virtualization-technical-specification.pdf`
   has a cover page reading *"INTEL SCALABLE I/O VIRTUALIZATION — Technical Specification —
   September 2020 — Order Number: 337679-002, Rev 1.1"*. Per CONVENTIONS §5 the PDF cover
   governs, so [21] should read **Rev 1.1**. CONVENTIONS §5's own line
   ("SIOV：337679-002 Rev 1.0（2020）") carries the same error and should be corrected with it.
   Entry [204] records the cover reading until [21] is fixed; once it is, [204] may be retired and
   its citations folded into [21].

2. **No Tier-3 sources are used by chapters 10–12.** Every register offset, bit position, fault code
   and structure layout in those three chapters was read directly out of the Rev 5.20 PDF
   (bibliography [8]) or, for chapter 09, out of the SDM Vol.3C and Vol.3D PDFs ([1], [2]).
   Entries [201] and [202] support only the 5520/5500 chipset erratum, which is anchored
   `[VT-d 5.20 Ch.?]` with `C:low` pending someone reading the Intel specification update itself.

3. **[200] and [203] are deliberately un-versioned.** Chapters 10–12 cite them only for stable
   structural facts (the PASID TLP Prefix exists and is 20 bits; ACPI description tables begin with a
   standard header). If a later chapter needs a version-specific PCIe or ACPI claim, allocate a new
   ID in the 41–99 range rather than pinning these.
