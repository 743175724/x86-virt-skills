## Notes for the merge

- **[256] is cited only to refute a misattribution.** INTEL-SA-01271 is a Gaudi software-installer
  permissions bug (CVE-2024-45067, CWE-276). It has nothing to do with TDX. Several secondary
  sources — including link text on the TDXRay project site itself — attribute TDXRay to it.
  `13-tdx.md §13.21.2` states the correction; keep the entry so the correction is citable.
- **[252]** has no arXiv preprint and no DOI that could be located. The IEEE Xplore DOI presumably
  exists but was not verified; do not invent one.
- **[251]** is an Intel support article rather than a specification. It is Tier 1 by
  `CONVENTIONS.md §3` (Intel official), but it is the *only* source for the per-generation AES key
  strength, and the TDX ISA specification itself fixes no key length. The claim citing it in
  `13-tdx.md §13.4.5` is deliberately `C:medium`.
- **[250]** corroborates the TDX-Module-version-to-platform binding, for which `[TDX-ABI §3.3.2]`
  is the Tier-1 anchor ("usually related to the supported SOCs").
- **[255]** the arXiv identifier 2602.11434 was checked against the arXiv API and is correct;
  an earlier note in the source material flagged it as suspect, and that suspicion was wrong.
