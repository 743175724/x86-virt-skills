Do not restore a dead Intel URL as a Tier-1 citation. There is no `[1103]` token in either chapter file.
- **[1106] is Tier 3** and is never the sole source for any claim; every claim citing it also carries a Tier-1 SDM anchor and, for HLAT, the Tier-2 Microsoft source [1107].
- **[1125] is Tier 3** and appears only in attributed form ("a third-party review reported…") and in the explicitly unresolved FlexPriority claim, which is marked `C:low`.
- Chapter 06 and chapter 07 each carry an explicit "facts deliberately not stated" paragraph at the end; those are the places where a future editor with the SDM PDF open should add content rather than assume the omission was accidental.
