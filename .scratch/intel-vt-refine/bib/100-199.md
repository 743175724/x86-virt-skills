## Notes for the merge

- **[105] is Tier 3** and is cited exactly once, on a `C:low` claim about the 2005-11-14 ship date of the Pentium 4 662/672, with the low confidence stated in the claim text. If a Tier-1 or Tier-2 Intel product source for that date is found, replace both the entry and the claim's confidence.
- **[102]'s URL was not verified.** The citation (journal, volume, issue, date) is solid; the Intel-hosted URL that the old source material carried no longer resolves reliably. Do not publish a guessed URL.
- **[106] is the cross-check source for the VMCS field-encoding tables in `02-vmcs.md` §2.5.** Every encoding in that section is stated against both the Volume 3D field-encoding appendix and this kernel header; encodings that the two could not jointly confirm were omitted and are listed in `02-vmcs.md` §2.5.6.
