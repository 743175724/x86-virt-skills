### Finding 2-A — WRONG (value) — body prose, not a claim line

- **File:line:** `amd-virtualization/references/02-vmcb.md:332`
- **Claim as written:**
  > Concretely, with `d` the 64-bit descriptor, `attrib = ((d >> 52) & 0xF00) | ((d >> 40) & 0xFF)`:
- **What the document says** (APM2 §15.5.1 "Segment State in the VMCB", PDF p.588, printed 525):
  > "segment attributes are stored as 12-bit values formed by the concatenation of bits 55:52 and
  > 47:40 from the original 64-bit (in-memory) segment descriptors"
- **Why it is wrong:** the high nibble must come from descriptor bits **55:52** landing at `attrib`
  bits 11:8. `(d >> 52) & 0xF00` masks bits 11:8 of the *shifted* value, i.e. descriptor bits
  **63:60** — part of the segment base, not the G/D/L/AVL nibble. The correct expression is
  `((d >> 44) & 0xF00)`, equivalently `(((d >> 52) & 0xF) << 8)`. The second term
  `((d >> 40) & 0xFF)` is correct.
- **Collateral note:** the surrounding sentence's bit mapping is right (attrib 3:0 Type, 4 S,
  6:5 DPL, 7 P, 8 AVL, 9 L, 10 D/B, 11 G), and the inverse direction stated one sentence later
  ("shifts bits 11:8 back to 55:52 and bits 7:0 back to 47:40") is also right. Only the forward
  shift constant is wrong. This is the single most copy-pasteable line in the chapter, so it is
  worth fixing even though it is not a `Claim` line.
- **Verdict: WRONG.**

### Finding 3-A — WRONG (the characterization contradicts the cited source)

- **File:line:** `03-intercepts.md:101`
- **Claim as written:** "The mapping is **segmented rather than positional** — CR reads at 00h, CR
  writes at 10h, DR at 20h/30h, exceptions at 40h, vector 3 at 60h + bit, vector 4 at 80h + bit and
  vector 5 at A0h + bit — which is why VMRUN (vector 4, bit 0) is 80h and the bus-lock exit
  (vector 5, bit 5) is A5h."  `[APM2 App.C]`
- **What the document says** (Appendix C preamble, APM2 PDF p.847, printed 784):
  > "Intercept exit codes are equal to the bit position of the corresponding flag in the VMCB's
  > intercept vector."
- **Why it is wrong:** the mapping *is* positional — it is exactly `32 × vector_index + bit`, and
  every base the chapter lists is a multiple of 32 (00h=0, 20h=32, 40h=64, 60h=96, 80h=128,
  A0h=160). The one sentence Appendix C uses to introduce the table asserts the opposite of
  "rather than positional".
- **Severity note, stated fairly:** every *value* in the claim is correct, and both worked examples
  are correct. The defect is the characterizing phrase. It also puts this chapter in direct
  conflict with `05-exit-decoding.md:21–22`, which states the rule correctly and completely
  ("`exit code = 32 × (vector number) + (bit position within that vector)`" and quotes the same
  Appendix C sentence). One of the two framings has to go, and chapter 05's is the right one.
- **Verdict: WRONG.**

### Finding 3-B — UNSUPPORTED

- **What the document says** (§15.12.2, APM2 p.603, printed 540):
