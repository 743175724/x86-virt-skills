## Chapter 08 — `08-iommu-architecture.md`

Checked against IOMMU-48882 Rev 3.11. TOC confirms five chapters, no appendices; no `App.`

### WRONG

**F08-1 — line 108. Universal statement about head/tail pointer registers is false for the GA log.**

> Claim as written: "Every head and tail pointer register carries its offset in bits 18:4, 16-byte
> aligned, with bits 3:0 and 63:19 reserved; writing a base-address register resets the matching head
> and tail pointers to zero, and head equal to tail means the buffer is empty.  `[IOMMU 3.11 §3.4.15]`"

What the document says (§3.4.18, MMIO Offset 2040h/2048h, PDF p.258–259):

> "Bits 63:16 — Reserved. **15:0 GALogHead**: Guest virtual APIC log head pointer. RW. Reset 0000h.
> Specifies the **64-bit aligned** offset from the Guest Virtual APIC Log Base Address Register
> [MMIO Offset 00E0h]…"
> "Bits 63:16 — Reserved. **15:0 GALogTail** … Specifies the **8-byte aligned** offset…"

And on the second half of the claim, §2.7.1 (p.189):

> "**Software is responsible to initialize the head and tail pointers to zero** to create an empty
> log before enabling guest vAPIC event logging."

— i.e. writing `GALogBase` does *not* clear them, unlike the event log (§2.5, p.140) and the PPR
log (§2.6, p.180) where the spec explicitly says the pointers "are cleared to 0".

So the sentence is wrong on two counts for the guest-vAPIC log pointer pair. It is correct for
the command buffer (2000h/2008h), event log (2010h/2018h), PPR log (2030h/2038h), PPR log B
(2050h/2058h) and event log B (2070h/2078h), all of which do use 18:4.

This also contradicts the chapter's own §8.8 claim at line 384, which correctly derives the GA log
full/empty condition from a shift-right-by-3 (8-byte) stride.

**Verdict: WRONG** (over-generalisation; fix by scoping to §3.4.15/3.4.17/3.4.19/3.4.20 and
excluding §3.4.18).

*Note in passing (not a chapter defect):* AMD's own diagrams for the PPR Log B head pointer
(2050h) and Event Log B head pointer (2070h) show the field at 15:3 while their Bits columns say
18:4. That inconsistency is AMD's, is not recorded in the chapter's Open items, and does not
change the chapter's text.

### WRONG

**F09-1 — line 397 (Open item 1). `INSERT_GUEST_EVENT`'s field offsets *are* determinable.**

> Claim as written: "**`INSERT_GUEST_EVENT` has no field-definition table.** §2.4.9 gives a figure

The first sentence is correct — §2.4.9 has no field table. The second is not: the unnumbered figure
in §2.4.9 (PDF p.137) gives the layout unambiguously. `pdftotext -layout`:

```
31       28 27                   16 15                             0
                               Reserved                               +00
    09h           Reserved                          GuestID[15:0]     +04
                               Reserved                               +08
                               Reserved                               +12
```

and `-raw` independently yields the same row order and the bit markers `28 27 / 15 16`. So
`GuestID[15:0]` is at dword **+04 bits 15:0** and the opcode 09h at **+04 bits 31:28** — the same
shape as `INVALIDATE_DEVTAB_ENTRY`/`INVALIDATE_INTERRUPT_TABLE` but with the ID moved to +04.
The Open item's claim of indeterminacy is false, and §9.4 therefore withholds information the
specification does supply.
**Verdict: WRONG.**

### IMPRECISE

**F14-1 — line 387. An unrecorded AMD contradiction about what the guest-message AUTHTAG covers.**

does not record the conflict. **Verdict: IMPRECISE** (the claim is faithful to its cited table;
the defect is the unrecorded contradiction, and the derived sentence "the header from the sequence
number onward is bound to the message" is the half that §8.27.2 undercuts).
